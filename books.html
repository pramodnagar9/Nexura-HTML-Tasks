Books I Love

Reading books helps us gain knowledge and develop new ideas.

Here are some of my favourite books that inspire me.

I enjoy reading books about personal growth and knowledge.

1. Atomic Habits

This book teaches us how small daily habits can bring big changes in life.

2. Bhagavad Gita

Bhagavad Gita teaches us about life, duty, self-discipline and staying calm in difficult situations.

3. Wings of Fire

This book tells the inspiring journey of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam from a simple background to becoming the President of India.

4. The Psychology of Money

This book explains money, saving and investing. It teaches us financial discipline and smart decisions.
