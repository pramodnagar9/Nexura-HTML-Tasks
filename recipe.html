1. Poha Jalebi

Poha Jalebi is a popular breakfast combination, especially in Madhya Pradesh. It is tasty and loved by many people.

Ingredients

  • Poha
  • Onion
  • Mustard Seeds
  • Oil and Spices
  • Jalebi

Preparation

  1. Wash the poha gently.
  2. Prepare a tadka with oil, mustard seeds and onion.
  3. Add poha and spices.
  4. Cook for a few minutes and serve with jalebi.

2. Dal Bati

Dal Bati is a traditional and delicious Indian dish. It is especially popular in Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh.

Ingredients

  • Wheat Flour
  • Toor Dal
  • Ghee
  • Salt and Spices

Preparation

  1. Prepare dough using wheat flour.
  2. Make small round balls and bake them.
  3. Cook dal with spices.
  4. Serve hot bati with dal and ghee.
